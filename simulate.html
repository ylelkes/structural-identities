About the simulated participants
This simulator uses 30 hand-crafted profiles representing a cross-section of American political types — strong progressives, union Democrats, libertarians, moderate Republicans, MAGA voters, and more. Each profile has a mean reaction time, a variability parameter, a political knowledge level (High / Medium / Low), and underlying valence scores derived from type-level political psychology research. High-knowledge participants respond more consistently with their ideological priors; low-knowledge participants add more noise. Individual responses are sampled probabilistically; within-person RT noise is drawn from a log-normal distribution. When you request more participants than 30, profiles are cycled and re-indexed.
