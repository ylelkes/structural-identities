About the simulated participants
This simulator uses 30 hand-crafted profiles representing a cross-section of American political types — strong progressives, union Democrats, libertarians, moderate Republicans, MAGA voters, and more. Each profile has a mean reaction time, a variability parameter, and a set of underlying valence scores (how positively or negatively they evaluate each concept) derived from type-level political psychology research. Individual responses are sampled probabilistically: valence determines whether a node is rated "good" or "bad" and whether a pair is rated "together" or "apart"; within-person RT noise is drawn from a log-normal distribution. When you request more participants than 30, profiles are cycled and re-indexed.
