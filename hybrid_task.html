Evaluation & Association Study

In this study you will complete a short computer task involving everyday words and concepts. You will respond using only the keyboard.

Your participation is voluntary and your responses are anonymous. The full task takes approximately 8–12 minutes.

By clicking "Begin" you agree to participate in this study.

Participant Information

Overview

What you will do

This study has two short parts.

In Part 1, you will see words or phrases appear on the screen one at a time. Press E or I to rate each one as GOOD or BAD.

In Part 2, you will see two items side-by-side and press a key to say whether they go together or do not go together in your mind.

Please respond as quickly and instinctively as you can — both speed and accuracy matter.

Part 1 — Evaluation Task

Instructions

You will see one word or phrase at a time. Your task is to judge whether it is GOOD or BAD.

Go with your first instinct — there are no trick questions. You must respond within seconds.

Part 2 — Association Task

Instructions

You will see two items appear side-by-side on the screen. Your task is to indicate whether these two things go together in your mind or do not go together.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are simply interested in your gut feeling — how mentally connected or associated the two items seem to you.

Please respond as quickly and instinctively as you can. You must respond within seconds.
